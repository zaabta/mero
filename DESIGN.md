## Brand & Style

The brand projects uncompromising engineering authority, motorsport precision, and Gulf luxury hospitality. Designed for high-net-worth automotive enthusiasts, fleet enterprise decision-makers, and discerning vehicle owners across Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates, the digital identity combines mechanical precision with bespoke luxury.

### Visual Movement: High-End Industrial Minimalist Luxury

The aesthetic draws directly from premium performance marques (such as Porsche and Michelin Pilot Sport engineering) blended with modern Arabian luxury:

- **Obsidian Dark Architecture:** Deep charcoal and carbon surfaces that echo matte tire compounds, performance composite brakes, and engine bay styling.
- **Precious Metallics:** Polished warm amber and satin brass gold highlights modeled on the signature metallic star emblem.
- **Editorial Cleanliness:** Generous negative space, sharp typographical hierarchies, and technical hairline dividers that mimic high-precision automotive tolerances.
- **Bilingual Prestige (RTL/LTR Equivalence):** Seamless architectural balance between Arabic and Latin scripts, maintaining identical visual weight and typographic authority across Arabic and English interfaces.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around pure obsidian depths, technical charcoal strata, and luminous warm golds.

### Palette Roles & Balance

- **Canvas & Surface Architecture:**
  - `#0B0C0E` serves as the primary canvas baseline (Deep Void Obsidian).
  - `#121417` forms primary card surfaces, elevated tiers, and structural container beds.
  - `#181B20` delivers elevated interactive surfaces, hover states, and dropdown components.
- **Metallic Gold Hierarchy:**
  - `#D4AF37` (Warm Satin Gold) is the primary interactive color for key CTA surfaces, focus strokes, and brand highlights.
  - `#E5B842` (Luminous Amber Gold) provides high-luminance hover states and active glow indicators.
  - `#C59A27` (Burnished Deep Gold) is utilized for subdued badge borders, inactive metallic accents, and technical tabular callouts.
- **Monochrome & Contrast:**
  - `#F5F5F7` (Crisp Ceramic White) ensures maximum legibility for primary headlines and data values.
  - `#A1A1AA` (Graphite Warm Gray) provides neutral contrast for secondary technical specifications, meta copy, and disabled states.
  - Hairline gold strokes are calibrated to `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.18)` on default states and `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.55)` on focus or elevated interaction.

## Typography

The typography pairs technical motorsport grit with sleek editorial discipline.

- **Headlines:** Set in `Space Grotesk` (paired with `IBM Plex Sans Arabic` in RTL environments) to evoke structural performance, tire tread geometry, and mechanical precision. Display weights utilize tight negative letter-spacing to enforce structural presence.
- **Body:** `Inter` handles long-form editorial copy, servicing descriptions, and technical specifications, providing clarity against the deep obsidian canvas.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** `IBM Plex Sans` uppercase styling delivers industrial serial code aesthetics, telemetry readouts, and badge identification.
- **RTL & Arabic Adaptations:** When rendering Arabic, headlines transition cleanly to `IBM Plex Sans Arabic` Bold/SemiBold without layout reflow, retaining baseline metrics and line heights that match the Latin scale.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded on a technical 12-column grid system built for structured luxury showcases, product tier comparisons, and automotive telemetry.

### Grid Rhythm & Adapting Breakpoints

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column architecture with generous `3rem` section margins and `1.5rem` internal gutters. Content is bounded at a maximum container width of `1380px` to mirror ultra-wide luxury editorial spreads.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` outer margins. Automotive specification tables collapse into 2-up comparison cards.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` outer canvas padding. Grids compress into single-column vertical cards, while action headers utilize persistent bottom anchor bars.
- **RTL Layout Flow:** The grid and spacing symmetry strictly mirror along the Y-axis. Leading gold accent strokes, back icons, and specification gauges dynamically swap alignment from left to right.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered matte carbon surfaces, ultra-refined gold hairlines, and restrained amber ambient back-lighting rather than generic diffuse black dropshadows.

### Elevation Architecture

- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** `#0B0C0E` base canvas, non-elevated.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Module Foundation):** `#121417` surfaced with a `1px` structural hairline border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` or `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.18)`.
- **Layer 2 (Floating Modals & Interactive Overlays):** `#181B20` with a subtle, directional gold rim shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 0 1px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`.
- **Layer 3 (Active Focus & Hero Highlights):** Subtle amber ambient bloom: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px 0 rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`.

### Materiality & Edge Treatments

To evoke high-end automotive instrument clusters and premium packaging:

- Glassmorphism is restrained to sticky navigation bars and telemetry chips using `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` over `#0B0C0E` at 85% opacity.
- Pure black borders are forbidden; borders always utilize satin gold or graphite hairlines to maintain structural separation in low-light viewports.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft industrial silhouette (`roundedness: 1`).

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px):** Applied to chips, badge tags, technical stat containers, and form input perimeters to preserve mechanical precision.
- **Container Radius (rounded-lg: 0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to main product showcase cards, booking modules, and interactive automotive panels.
- **Outer Shell Radius (rounded-xl: 0.75rem / 12px):** Reserved for floating dialogs, system popovers, and hero banner enclosures.
- Avoid hyper-rounded or fully pill-shaped buttons in corporate contexts to maintain the engineered, industrial aesthetic inspired by automotive component chassis.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs

- **Primary Gold Button:** Solid metallic fill `#D4AF37` with crisp `#0B0C0E` bold typography. Hover state shifts to `#E5B842` with an ambient `0 0 16px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)` glow. Padding: `12px 24px`. Border radius: `4px`.
- **Secondary Carbon Button:** `#181B20` surface with a `1px` border in `#D4AF37` and `#F5F5F7` text. On hover, the border brightens to `#E5B842` with background tinting to `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`.
- **Ghost Action:** High-contrast text `#F5F5F7` with subtle uppercase label tracking and an animated trailing gold arrow or directional caret.

### Cards & Product Modules

- **Tire & Parts Spec Card:** Background in `#121417`, defined by a `1px` perimeter border of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`. Upper section features performance tire visual with soft spotlight gradient; lower half holds split specs (Speed Rating, Load Index, Treadwear) rendered in `#A1A1AA` with values in `#F5F5F7`.
- **Service Package Tier Card:** Features an optional top gold ribbon badge featuring the corporate five-point star icon.

### Chips & Badges

- **Technical Status Badge:** Background `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.1)` with a `1px` border of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`, text in `#E5B842` uppercase (`10px` / `0.1em` tracking).
- **Metric Indicator Chip:** Compact `#181B20` container with contrasting mono values for tire compound heat dissipation, warranty mileage, and oil viscosity grades (e.g., `5W-30`).

### Input Fields & Selectors

- **Form Controls:** Background `#0B0C0E`, `1px` perimeter in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text `#F5F5F7`.
- **Focus State:** Hairline transitions to sharp `#D4AF37` with a non-intrusive outer ring of `0 0 0 2px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`. Labels float with uppercase IBM Plex Sans styling.

### Automotive Specific Components

- **Tread & Specification Comparison Grid:** Dark table rows separated by `1px` horizontal lines (`#181B20`), highlighting vehicle fitment compatibility for GCC spec conditions (heat endurance, sand grip, high-speed stability).
- **Service Center Locator:** Interactive dark map integration with custom gold star map markers and localized bilingual opening hours for Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, and Dubai branches.
